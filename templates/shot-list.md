Use a different visual ID for every planned shot. Do not place the same source clip, screenshot, crop, or composition in adjacent shots. For a sentence longer than about four seconds, plan at least two distinct story visuals and tie each to a clause or narrative beat. Plan a rendered pilot containing representative movement and at least one join. Record whether it was actually played or sampled. Do not count decorative motion, image panning, or a progress bar as proof that filmed action exists.
