# Visual Beat Sheet

Use this for motion-led explainers, promos, data stories, animated sequences, and other videos where visuals should perform ideas over time. A beat can also be a deliberate hold when stillness best serves the message.

**Motion thesis:** What single visual metaphor or changing state connects the film?

**Visual system:** Palette, type, framing, motion language, transition rules, and sound rhythm.

| Beat | Script / audio range | Viewer takeaway | Opening visual state | Change or action | Ending state / handoff | Source or asset needed | Sound cue | Readability / risk notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | 00:00–00:05 | Example: understand the problem | A crowded, hard-to-read screen | Elements clear one by one | One highlighted issue leads into the solution | Approved product capture | Soft reveal, no voice masking | Keep UI evidence genuine |

## Review before full production

- [ ] Every visual action clarifies a line, claim, emotion, or transition.
- [ ] The visual state changes over time when the treatment promises motion.
- [ ] The sequence forms one connected idea rather than unrelated animated cards.
- [ ] Captions, titles, charts, and controls remain legible at intended playback size.
- [ ] Motion preserves accurate data and does not imply a false comparison or causal claim.
- [ ] A representative sample has been reviewed before scaling production.
