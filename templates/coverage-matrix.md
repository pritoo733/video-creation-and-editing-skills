## Final completeness check

- [ ] Every required point from the brief appears in the script and final export.
- [ ] Every factual claim has a source or is clearly labeled as inference/opinion.
- [ ] On-screen wording matches approved copy and source facts.
- [ ] The opening establishes the subject and viewer promise.
- [ ] The ending resolves the promise and includes the requested next step.
- [ ] Any omitted, changed, or unresolved item is recorded and disclosed to the reviewer.
