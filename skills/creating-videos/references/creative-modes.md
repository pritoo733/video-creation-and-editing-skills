# Choose a Creative Mode

Start with the user's goal and available material. These modes can be combined.

| Mode | Useful starting point | Main review question |
|---|---|---|
| Personal story or photo video | Meaningful real memory, photos, short narration | Does it feel specific and true to the people involved? |
| Product or software walkthrough | Audience, role, verified workflow, genuine capture plan | Can a new user follow the actions and limits? |
| Explainer or educational video | Viewer question, source-backed outline, diagrams | Does each visual clarify the spoken point? |
| Marketing or brand video | Offer, audience, proof, desired next step | Are claims supported and assets cleared for commercial use? |
| Social short | One idea, strong opening, focused payoff | Is the message understandable on a phone with sound off? |
| Presenter or talking-head explainer | Approved script/audio, presentation route, supporting visuals | Is the person natural and in sync, and do cutaways clarify each beat? |
| Footage edit or event recap | Source review, story beats, clean audio moments | Do cuts preserve chronology, context, and important moments? |
| AI-assisted visual story | Visual treatment, prompts, continuity reference | Are artifacts acceptable, and could viewers mistake generated shots for evidence? |
| Interview | Strong complete statements, supporting B-roll | Does the edit preserve speaker meaning and sync? |

For personal material, establish the intended feeling before selecting music, pacing, effects, or captions. Use supplied memories rather than inventing drama. For advertising or factual video, verify material claims and distinguish illustration from evidence. For how-to content, use `app-capture.md` for live software capture.
